How the score is calculated

Ratings run from 1 (evidence of poor fit) to 5 (strong evidence of fit). Unknown dimensions are excluded from the average. Coverage is the share of rubric weight supported by evidence. The demo uses editorial judgment to determine whether coverage is sufficient for a proposed tier.

Proposed tiers are Strong (80 and above), Promising (65 to below 80), Mixed (50 to below 65), and Weak (below 50). An insufficient evidence packet gets an Unknown proposed tier. The editor reviews the evidence before deciding whether to include a brand.
